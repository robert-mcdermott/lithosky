WELCOME TO LITHOSKY

Find your next horizon.

Choose a study, change its seed, then shape the mountain height and light. Everything you see is generated locally.

  • Start with a scene from the menu. Try Alpine quiet, Evening highlands, or A distant world. A scene replaces the current composition; Undo brings it back.
  • Drag left/right to turn around the landscape; drag up/down to raise or lower your view. Scroll to move closer or farther away.
  • With the view focused, use arrow keys to orbit and + / − to zoom. Home resets the camera.
  • After Build with layers, choose Raise, Lower, Smooth or Flatten under Sculpt terrain. Drag on land and release to apply one stroke. Arrow keys move the brush; Space applies it; Escape cancels. Flatten uses the height where the stroke starts. Switch back to Orbit camera to turn your view.
  • For your first still: choose a scene, orbit until the mountain silhouette feels right, set the water level, then choose Render image. Save scene keeps the composition; Export PNG keeps the picture.
  • Open Graphics & compatibility to select a preview backend or retry after a graphics failure.

Open and Save scene use .lithosky JSON files. Autosave keeps local recovery snapshots between launches. Use Undo/Redo (Ctrl/⌘ Z and Ctrl/⌘ Shift Z) for scene and camera edits. Saving a scene downloads a new file; it does not update an earlier download. Use Render image for a progressive 16:9 still with traced terrain, sun/sky lighting and calm-water reflection/refraction. Cancel keeps the last complete sample. Export PNG downloads the displayed image in sRGB. Changes to the landscape clear the render. Scene names do not. Build with layers creates a new terrain with rolling hills, ridges, domain warp and terraces. Reorder layers to change their effect; use Undo to return to your original scene. Generation runs in the background and can be canceled. Generation grids reach 2048 × 2048 cells; the preview and still use a 128 × 128 mesh in this build. Final rendering requires WebGPU; WebGL 2 remains available for preview. Physical atmosphere, waves and vegetation come in later releases.
