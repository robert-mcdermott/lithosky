THE WORKSPACE

Your scene.
Your pace.

A local studio with an interactive preview, undo and recovery. Final rendering runs on your GPU through WebGPU.

SMALL BY DESIGN

A beginning
you can build on.

A single Go executable brings the studio to your device. An installed compatible browser supplies its desktop-style window.

The first renderer traces bounded terrain, sun/sky lighting, and calm-water reflection and refraction. It exports stills up to 960 × 540. WebGL 2 supplies a fallback preview.

Physical atmosphere, clouds, vegetation and larger exports are future work. The images here show what the app renders today.
