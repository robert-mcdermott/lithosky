# Lithosky 0.5.0 — First Light

The first public release of Lithosky, a free landscape studio that runs on your own
computer. It makes landscape pictures worth sharing: eroded mountains, canyon country,
luminous skies and real clouds, path traced on your GPU.

## Highlights

- **Terrain shaped by water.** Rivers carve valley networks, droplets cut gullies, and
  scree piles below cliffs. All of it is simulated on the GPU in a few seconds.
- **Two landforms.**
  - Alpine ranges with glacial lakes.
  - Canyon country: plateaus broken into mesas and buttes, with every slope stepping
    into cliffs and ledges banded by its rock layers.
- **A physical sky.** Sunlight scatters through the atmosphere, and haze deepens with
  distance. The air's colour runs from deep blue to an alien gold.
- **Volumetric clouds.** From fair-weather heaps to storm towers with anvils. They glow
  at their edges, cast shadows on the land and show in the water.
- **Ground and water from the landform.** Rock, scree, meadow, forest, sand and snow go
  where the land puts them, across five biomes. Lakes ripple in the wind.
- **A viewport-first studio.**
  - The picture fills the window, and seven labs open beside it.
  - Galleries show your own view with each choice applied.
  - Drag any value to adjust it, and drag the sun across a sky dome.
  - Press Render and watch the final image refine in place.
- **Framing.** Image shapes from 16:9 to 4:5 portrait, thirds and golden-ratio guides,
  and saved camera views.
- **Thirteen built-in scenes** to start from.

## Requirements

- macOS, Windows or Linux on Intel/AMD or ARM.
- A recent Chromium-family browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Chromium) with WebGPU and
  graphics acceleration. The browser draws the window; it isn't bundled.

## Install

Download the archive for your system, extract it, and run `lithosky` (or `lithosky.exe`
on Windows). Packages aren't signed yet, so your system may ask you to approve the app
the first time. `SHA256SUMS` lists each archive's checksum.

## Good to know

- Scene files use format version 1, which is now frozen: later versions keep opening
  them.
- Lithosky is local and private. There's no account, no telemetry and nothing
  downloaded at run time.
- Two renders of the same saved scene can differ very slightly in fine terrain detail.
- Vegetation (trees and grass), rocks and objects, oceans, and night skies are planned
  next.
