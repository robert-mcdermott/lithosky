# Lithosky 0.6.0 — Forests and meadows

Lithosky's landscapes now grow forests, shrubs and grass, placed by the land itself and
path traced down to single leaves and blades up close. Lithosky also installs and
upgrades with one line.

## Highlights

- **Forests.** Conifers and broadleaf trees grow where a tree could stand: off cliffs,
  scree and stream beds, in stands broken by clearings, smaller towards the tree line,
  where conifers take over. They cast soft shadows, darken the forest floor and show in
  the lakes.
- **Shrubs and meadow grass.** Shrubs fill the gaps between stands and the belt around
  the tree line, and grow far above it in dry country. Near the camera, meadows are real
  grass: tussocks and single blades leaning with the wind, glowing when backlit.
- **Close-up foliage.** Near trees are made of individual leaves and needle clusters.
  Light comes through the gaps in their crowns.
- **The Plants lab**, the eighth lab:
  - a gallery of looks rendered from your own view: Natural, Conifer forest, Broadleaf
    woods, Meadows, Scrubland and Bare
  - sliders for forest, tree size, tree mix, shrubs and grass
  - Randomize
- **Paint vegetation in the view.** Pick Plant, Clear or Restore in the Plants lab and
  drag across the land. Plant forest along a shore, or clear a meadow. Each stroke is
  one undo step and is saved with the scene.
- **A calmer live view.** The view stops tracing once it's done and does nothing while
  the window is hidden. **Draft** (the default) refines in a second or two; **Full**
  matches the final render. Switch between them in the top bar or with F. Render still
  makes the full-quality image.
- **A new scene: Meadow, evening.** Fourteen built-in scenes now, all showing their
  vegetation.
- **One-line installers.** Each release is now a standalone executable for each system,
  plus a `SHA256SUMS` manifest. The installers check the download against it before
  installing, with no admin rights needed.

## Requirements

- macOS, Windows or Linux on Intel/AMD or ARM.
- A recent Chromium-family browser (Chrome, Edge, Brave or Chromium) with WebGPU and
  graphics acceleration. The browser draws the window; it isn't bundled.

## Upgrading from 0.5.0

0.5.0 shipped as archives, so the installers can't upgrade it in place. Install 0.6.0
with the commands below, then delete the folder you extracted 0.5.0 into. Your scenes
and recovery snapshots stay where they were.

## Good to know

- Scenes from 0.5.0 open unchanged and gain the vegetation of their biome. Plant
  settings and paint are saved in a new, optional part of the scene (format version 1,
  as before). 0.5.0 can't open scenes that use them.
- Painting covers the central 16 km of the terrain. Beyond that, the forest follows the
  land.
- Vegetation adds render time, most of all on forested scenes.
- Two renders of the same saved scene can differ very slightly in fine terrain detail,
  and the plants move with it.
- Programs aren't signed with an Apple or Microsoft certificate yet. The installers
  check every download against its published checksum instead.
- Rocks and objects, oceans and coastlines, and night skies are planned next.
